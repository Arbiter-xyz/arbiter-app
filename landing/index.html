How it works

  1. 01

    Ask

    POST the claim you need checked to /oracle — an audit finding, a contract's behavior, an on-chain event. No API key, no signup.

  2. 02

    Pay

    The easy way: pay by card — no wallet, no XLM, no setup. If you already have a Stellar wallet, you can instead submit USDC to escrow (the platform covers your network fee, zero XLM needed) or use an API key billed by the question.

  3. 03

    Answer

    A live quorum of online workers gets your question over a real-time channel and races to answer it.

  4. 04

    Settle

    Answers are reconciled into one consensus. Paid out automatically on-chain — or fully refunded, never partially.

Why Arbiter

Every guarantee below is enforced by the smart contract itself, not a policy we promise to follow.

Fail-closed by design

If a quorum can't be reached, the contract refunds the full amount. There is no partial-payout state to get stuck in.

Real-time quorum

Workers receive questions over a live channel and race to answer, so most claims settle in seconds, not hours.

Pay how you want

Card, USDC escrow, or an API key billed per question. No wallet required to get started.

Developers
